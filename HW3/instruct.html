Step 1 - open this beatmaker. i suggest pasting it into a new tab so you can reference these instructions

Step 2 - click all of the colored boxes so your drumpad is blank

Step 3 - scroll down to the instruments, and delete 3 of the 4 the website has given you

Step 4 - find the 808 kick drum on the drumpad(orange one on the bottom). Add a kick to boxes, 1, 5, 9, 12, & 15 from left to right

Step 5 - locate the dropdown button that says "1 bar" and switch it to "2 bars", repeat the previous step on the new, empty, 16 boxes to the right

Step 6 - click the purple icon on the left with the hand that says "light", switch it to snaps, and add it to boxes 27 & 29

Step 7 - locate the "bpm" button and type in a number anywhere from 130-160.





Congrats! You have just made a super simple jersey club beat :)


Bonus Step - make the beat your own! add in cymbals, snares, hi-hats, open hats, etc. Add some instruments of your liking and mess around with the chords & key.
